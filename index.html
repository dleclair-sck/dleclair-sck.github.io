Cost segregation, made approachable

Underwrite the after-tax return—not only the top line.

For high-net-worth investors, gross revenue is only part of the investment thesis. Cost segregation is an engineering-based study that may reclassify eligible property components into shorter depreciable lives, potentially accelerating depreciation and improving near-term tax efficiency.

We surface cost-segregation potential early—alongside acquisition basis, planned CapEx, projected cash flow, and hold strategy—so qualified tax professionals can evaluate its place in your broader portfolio and capital-allocation plan.

Results depend on the property and your individual tax situation. We coordinate with qualified cost-segregation and tax professionals; we do not provide tax or legal advice.

Why include renovation and furnishing in the underwriting?
